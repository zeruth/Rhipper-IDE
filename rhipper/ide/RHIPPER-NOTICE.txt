- They added the Rhipper product: its application info, icons, splash image and installer images, and its build

- They added the Rhipper plugin: Rhipper projects, the pipeline, the corpus and fidelity views, clangd support,
  MCP tools, the tools setup and the Rhipper skill.
- The splash image stays on top of other windows, stays visible for at least four seconds, and can show the
  product build and the platform version (platform/platform-impl, Splash.kt and SplashManager.kt).
- The dev-distribution generator builds the Rhipper product (communityDevDistHalf.kt).
